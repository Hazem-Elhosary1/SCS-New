one it replaces, and the owner is told by email when a reset is completed.

## Overview

Spec 003 lets a person sign in — if they have a password and remember it. This spec covers
everything about getting and changing that password without help from support:

1. **Forgot password**: ask for a reset link by email, and choose a new password from it.
2. **A new account**: the person invited by a link chooses their first password, which
   activates the account.
3. **A temporary password**: the person whose account was handed over with a password someone
   else chose must replace it at first sign-in, before reaching anything else.
4. **Links that no longer work**: expired, used or replaced links say so, and say what to do.

Throughout, the rule of spec 003 holds: nothing shown or sent confirms to a stranger that an
account exists.

It also gives later specs the operations they need to hand over a new account in each of the
three ways the BRD allows (SH-044): a link by email, a password generated by the system and shown
once, or a password typed by whoever creates the account.

**Out of scope** (each has its own spec): the sign-in form, the email code and sessions (003);
permissions and roles (005); the console shell (006); the audit trail's pages (007); the
notifications engine and email templates in general (008); operator passwords (010); inviting a
clinic's owner from the operators console (015); creating, editing and disabling team accounts
and choosing the delivery method on a screen (026); the clinic's activity and security log page
(059); the password policy as a clinic setting (085); changing one's own password from inside
the console (085).

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-10

- Q: When someone sets a new password, must it be different from the one it replaces? → A: Yes — a new password equal to the current one is refused. This is what makes changing a temporary password mean something.
- Q: When a password reset is completed, should the account's owner get an email saying the password was changed? → A: Yes — a short notice, with advice to contact support if it was not them.
- Q: Beyond one reset email per account per minute, should there be a longer cap on reset emails for one account? → A: Yes — at most 5 per account per hour; requests beyond it get the same answer and send nothing.
- Q: Should this spec also provide the operation that hands over an existing account again, or only the hand-over at creation? → A: It provides it — one operation re-issues access for an existing account by a new temporary password (generated or typed) or by a link.
- Q: Should a temporary password stop working if its owner never signs in with it? → A: Yes — it expires 72 hours after it was set; after that, sign-in gets the usual wrong-credentials answer.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - A person who forgot their password gets back in by themselves (Priority: P1)

A doctor cannot remember his password. Under the sign-in button he presses «نسيت كلمة المرور؟»,
types his email and presses «إرسال رابط إعادة التعيين». The screen says «أُرسل الطلب» and that
if the address is registered a link will arrive within minutes. He opens the link from his
email, sees «تعيين كلمة مرور جديدة» for his account, types a new password while four lines turn
green one by one, and presses «تعيين كلمة المرور الجديدة». He is back on the sign-in form with
«غُيّرت كلمة المرور». He signs in with both steps as usual.

**Why this priority**: without it every forgotten password is a call to support or to the
clinic's manager, and a locked account stays locked for fifteen minutes with no way out.

**Independent Test**: for a made-up active account, request a link, open it from the captured
email, set a new password, and sign in with it; confirm the old password no longer works and
that no session was opened by the reset itself.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the sign-in form, **When** «نسيت كلمة المرور؟» is pressed, **Then** the form titled
   «إعادة تعيين كلمة المرور» is shown with one email field and the button «إرسال رابط إعادة
   التعيين».
2. **Given** the email of an active account, **When** the button is pressed, **Then** the result
   screen reads «أُرسل الطلب» and «إن كان ‹البريد› مسجَّلًا في العيادة فسيصله خلال دقائق رابطٌ
   لإعادة تعيين كلمة المرور.», and a reset link is in that email.
3. **Given** an empty email, **When** the button is pressed, **Then** «البريد مطلوب» appears
   under the field with no banner and no call; a malformed email shows «البريد غير صحيح — مثل
   name@clinic.com».
4. **Given** a live reset link, **When** it is opened, **Then** the screen reads «تعيين كلمة مرور
   جديدة» and «لحساب ‹البريد›», with one field «كلمة المرور الجديدة», the eye, and the four
   rules shown before anything is typed.
5. **Given** that screen, **When** a password meeting all four rules is typed and «تعيين كلمة
   المرور الجديدة» is pressed, **Then** the sign-in form is shown with the success banner «غُيّرت
   كلمة المرور» / «الدخول الآن بكلمة المرور الجديدة.», and no session exists.
6. **Given** the reset is done, **When** he signs in, **Then** the new password works with both
   steps, and the old one gets the usual wrong-credentials answer.
7. **Given** a locked account, **When** its owner completes a reset, **Then** the lock is gone
   and he signs in without waiting.
8. **Given** the account had live sessions on other devices, **When** the reset is completed,
   **Then** every one of them is ended.
9. **Given** the reset is completed, **When** the owner checks his email, **Then** a notice says
   the password was changed and what to do if it was not him.
10. **Given** either form, **When** «الرجوع لتسجيل الدخول» is pressed, **Then** the sign-in form
    is shown.

### User Story 2 - Asking for a link never reveals whether an account exists (Priority: P1)

Someone types email after email into the "forgot password" form to find out who works at a
clinic. Every request ends on the same «أُرسل الطلب» screen, after the same wait — for a
registered address, an unregistered one, a disabled account, an account never activated, a
second request a few seconds after the first, and even when the email could not be sent.

**Why this priority**: it is the same constitution rule as the sign-in form (SH-043). A reset
form that says "no such account" undoes it.

**Independent Test**: send the request for each of those cases and compare the answers and their
timing; they cannot be told apart. Check the captured mail: only the active account received
anything, and only once per minute.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an address that belongs to no account, **When** a link is requested, **Then** the
   same result screen is shown, letter for letter, and no email is sent.
2. **Given** a disabled account, or an invited account that was never activated, **When** a link
   is requested, **Then** the same screen is shown and no email is sent.
3. **Given** a link was sent to an account less than a minute ago, or five were sent to it in
   the last hour, **When** another is requested, **Then** the same screen is shown, no email is
   sent, and the last link sent still works.
4. **Given** the email service is failing, **When** a link is requested for an active account,
   **Then** the same screen is shown.
5. **Given** all of the cases above, **When** the answers are compared, **Then** they are
   identical apart from the request reference, and their response times cannot be told apart.
6. **Given** more than the allowed number of requests from one network address in a minute,
   **When** another is sent, **Then** a warning on the same form says when to try again.

### User Story 3 - A new team member activates their account from a link (Priority: P1)

A new receptionist receives an email with a link. She opens it and reads «مرحبًا بك في ‹اسم
العيادة›» and «اختر كلمة مرور لحسابك ‹البريد›». She types a password, watching the four rules
turn green, sees the note «الرابط صالح ٧٢ ساعة من إرساله.», and presses «تفعيل الحساب». The
sign-in form appears with «فُعِّل الحساب»; she signs in with both steps.

**Why this priority**: the link is the default way an account is handed over (SH-044), and the
way every clinic's first account — its owner's — comes to life.

**Independent Test**: create an invited account with a link through the operation this spec
provides, open the link from the captured email, set a password, and sign in.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an invited account and its live link, **When** the link is opened, **Then** the
   screen greets with the clinic's name in the current language, names the account's email, and
   shows the field, the four rules and the note about 72 hours.
2. **Given** that screen, **When** a valid password is typed and «تفعيل الحساب» is pressed,
   **Then** the account becomes active, the sign-in form is shown with the success banner
   «فُعِّل الحساب» / «الدخول الآن بكلمة المرور الجديدة.», and no session exists.
3. **Given** the account was activated, **When** she signs in, **Then** both steps work as for
   any active account.
4. **Given** an invited account, **When** it is created with a link, **Then** an email carries
   the link, the clinic's name, the sign-in address and the 72-hour limit.
5. **Given** an invited account whose link was lost, **When** whoever may do so sends it again,
   **Then** a new link arrives and the earlier one stops working.
6. **Given** an invited account, **When** its email is typed into "forgot password", **Then**
   nothing is sent (the answer is the same screen as always).

### User Story 4 - A link that no longer works says so, and says what to do (Priority: P2)

A person opens a link that is too old, was already used, or was replaced by a newer one. Instead
of a form they see «انتهت صلاحية الرابط أو استُعمل». For a reset link the screen explains that
it works once and for a limited time, and offers «طلب رابط جديد». For a new-account link it says
that sending another is up to whoever sent the first — and offers no button, because "forgot
password" sends nothing to an account that was never activated.

**Why this priority**: a dead link that shows an error page, or a form that fails only on
submit, leaves people stuck; the wrong button here is a silent dead end.

**Independent Test**: open a reset link after 30 minutes, after use, and after a newer one was
issued; open a new-account link after 72 hours and after use; open a made-up link. Each shows
the right screen and nothing can be set.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a reset link older than 30 minutes, **When** it is opened, **Then** «انتهت صلاحية
   الرابط أو استُعمل» is shown with «رابط إعادة تعيين كلمة المرور يعمل مرة واحدة ولمدة محدودة.»
   and the button «طلب رابط جديد», which leads to the request form.
2. **Given** a reset link that was used once, **When** it is opened again, **Then** the same
   screen is shown.
3. **Given** a newer reset link was issued for the account, **When** the earlier one is opened,
   **Then** the same screen is shown.
4. **Given** a new-account link older than 72 hours, or already used, **When** it is opened,
   **Then** the title is the same, the message reads «رابط الحساب الجديد يعمل مرة واحدة ولمدة
   محدودة. إرسال رابط آخر بيد من أرسل الأول.», and there is no button.
5. **Given** a link that was never issued, or was altered, **When** it is opened, **Then** the
   reset-link screen is shown; nothing tells it apart from an expired one.
6. **Given** a link that was live when the page opened, **When** it expires before the password
   is submitted, **Then** the same "no longer works" screen replaces the form and nothing is
   changed.
7. **Given** any of these screens, **When** «الرجوع لتسجيل الدخول» is pressed, **Then** the
   sign-in form is shown.

### User Story 5 - The password rules are shown, and enforced by the server (Priority: P2)

Wherever a new password is chosen there is one field and four lines under it: «١٠ أحرف على
الأقل», «حرف كبير وحرف صغير (A-z)», «رقم واحد على الأقل (0-9)», «رمز خاص واحد على الأقل
(!@#$%)». Each line is neutral before typing, turns green when met, and turns red on submit if
it is not. There is no second "confirm password" field: the eye is the check.

**Why this priority**: a rule shown only as an error after submit costs several tries; a rule
checked only in the browser is not a rule.

**Independent Test**: on each of the three screens, try passwords that break each rule in turn;
then send such passwords directly to the server, bypassing the screen.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a set-password screen, **When** nothing is typed yet, **Then** the four lines are
   shown in their neutral state.
2. **Given** the screen, **When** a password of nine characters is submitted, **Then** the line
   «١٠ أحرف على الأقل» turns red, no error appears under the field, the button stays enabled,
   and nothing is changed.
3. **Given** the screen, **When** a password with no symbol is submitted, **Then** the line
   «رمز خاص واحد على الأقل (!@#$%)» turns red.
4. **Given** the screen, **When** the characters typed meet a rule, **Then** its line turns
   green as they are typed.
5. **Given** a password that breaks a rule is sent straight to the server, **When** it is
   received, **Then** it is refused naming each unmet rule, and nothing is changed.
6. **Given** the new password equals the account's current one, **When** it is submitted,
   **Then** it is refused with a message saying a different password is needed, and nothing is
   changed.
7. **Given** any set-password screen, **When** it is inspected, **Then** there is exactly one
   password field, with the eye.

### User Story 6 - A temporary password must be replaced at first sign-in (Priority: P2)

A clinic manager creates an account for a nurse and gives her a password — one the system
generated and showed once, or one the manager typed. The nurse gets an email saying she has an
account and where to sign in; the password is not in it. She signs in with both steps and,
before any page of the console, is shown the set-password screen with a title saying the
password is temporary. Only after choosing her own password does she get in. The next time she
signs in, she is not asked.

**Why this priority**: a password known to two people is not a credential. This is two of the
three ways an account is handed over (SH-044).

**Independent Test**: create an account with a temporary password through this spec's operation;
sign in; confirm every other request is refused until the password is changed; change it; sign
in again and confirm nothing is asked.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an account created with a generated password, **When** it is created, **Then** it
   is active, the password is returned to the creator exactly once, and it is marked "must
   change at first sign-in".
2. **Given** an account created with a typed password, **When** it is created, **Then** the
   same holds, and the typed password itself must meet the four rules.
3. **Given** either, **When** it is created, **Then** the account's owner receives an email
   saying an account exists and giving the sign-in address — and not the password.
4. **Given** such an account, **When** its owner completes both sign-in steps, **Then** the
   set-password screen is shown instead of the console, with a title saying the password is
   temporary, one field and the four rules.
5. **Given** she is on that screen, **When** she tries to open any other page or make any other
   request, **Then** she is brought back to it; nothing of the console is reachable. Signing out
   remains possible.
6. **Given** that screen, **When** she submits a password that meets the rules and differs from
   the temporary one, **Then** she reaches the console in the same session, and the mark is
   removed.
7. **Given** she changed it, **When** she next signs in, **Then** the screen is not shown.
8. **Given** that screen, **When** she submits the temporary password itself, **Then** it is
   refused and the mark stays.
9. **Given** an account with a temporary password, **When** its owner uses "forgot password" and
   completes a reset instead, **Then** the mark is removed: she has chosen her own password.
10. **Given** a temporary password set more than 72 hours ago and never replaced, **When** its
    owner tries to sign in with it, **Then** the answer is the usual wrong-credentials one, and
    nothing says the password expired.
11. **Given** she is already on the set-password screen when the 72 hours pass, **When** she
    submits her own password, **Then** it is accepted: the limit is on signing in with the
    temporary password, not on finishing the change.
12. **Given** an existing active account, **When** access is re-issued with a new temporary
    password, **Then** the old password stops working, the account's sessions end, its links
    stop working, it is marked "must change", and its owner gets an email saying access was
    reset — without the password.
13. **Given** an existing active account, **When** access is re-issued by link, **Then** a reset
    link is emailed and the current password keeps working until the link is used.

### User Story 7 - Every one of these screens is complete and bilingual (Priority: P3)

All of these screens use the frame of the sign-in screens: the introduction panel, the form, the
language switch, and the footer with the help link and the links to the privacy policy and the
terms. Every state exists in Arabic and in English.

**Why this priority**: constitution VI. These are screens people meet when something has already
gone wrong.

**Independent Test**: open each state in both languages and check the frame, the mirrored
layout, the footer, the help window, and that no clinic name is shown except on the new-account
screen.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** any of these screens in Arabic, **When** the language switch is pressed, **Then**
   it is in English with the panel on the other side.
2. **Given** any of these screens, **When** the help link is pressed, **Then** the help window
   of spec 003 opens.
3. **Given** the request form, its result, the reset screen and both "no longer works" screens,
   **When** they are inspected, **Then** no clinic's name is shown.
4. **Given** the new-account screen, **When** it is inspected, **Then** the clinic's name is
   shown, because the holder of the link is already identified by it.
5. **Given** any of these screens while the platform cannot be reached, **When** an action is
   tried, **Then** a banner says so and what to do; the page is never blank.

### Edge Cases

- The same reset link is submitted twice at the same moment (double press): the password is set
  once; the second submission sees the "no longer works" screen.
- Two reset requests for one account arrive at the same moment: one link is live afterwards, and
  at most one email is sent.
- A reset is requested for a locked account: the link is sent; completing it lifts the lock.
- A reset link is live, and the account is disabled before it is used: the link no longer works.
- A reset link is live, and the person signs in successfully with their old password: the link
  stays live until it expires or is used.
- A reset link is live, and the clinic is suspended: the password can still be set; signing in
  afterwards is refused as spec 003 says.
- A new-account link is live, and the account is disabled or removed before it is used: the link
  no longer works.
- A new-account link is opened by someone who is signed in as another account on the same
  browser: the screen is for the link's account; the existing session is not used and is not
  ended.
- The link is opened in an email preview or by a link scanner before the person clicks it:
  opening a link changes nothing; only submitting a password uses it up.
- The link's address is shared, bookmarked or left in browser history: the address is cleaned as
  soon as the page has read it, and the link works once.
- The person with a temporary password closes the browser on the set-password screen: at the
  next sign-in the screen is shown again.
- The person with a temporary password never signs in: after 72 hours it stops working. The
  account stays active, so its owner can still use "forgot password", or the creator can hand
  the account over again.
- A wrong try with an expired temporary password counts toward the lock like any other wrong
  password.
- The password typed contains Arabic letters, spaces or emoji: accepted when the four rules are
  met; a rule counts what it names (a symbol is a punctuation mark or a symbol character; a
  space is neither, and an emoji does not count as one).
- The password is longer than 200 characters: refused.
- The generated password: meets the four rules itself, and avoids characters that are easily
  confused when read aloud or copied by hand.
- The email service fails when a new-account link is sent: the operation reports it to its
  caller, so the creator knows to send again; the account stays invited.
- The email service fails when a reset link is sent: nothing changes — the link sent before,
  if any, still works.
- The email service fails when the "password changed" notice is sent: the reset still stands;
  the failure is logged.
- A reset is completed while the person is signed in elsewhere: those sessions end and their
  next action shows the sign-in form with the neutral "session ended" notice.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Asking for a reset link**

- **FR-001**: The sign-in form's «نسيت كلمة المرور؟» MUST open a form titled «إعادة تعيين كلمة
  المرور», with the line «أدخل بريدك الإلكتروني وسنرسل لك رابط إعادة تعيين كلمة المرور», one
  email field, the button «إرسال رابط إعادة التعيين» and the link «الرجوع لتسجيل الدخول».
- **FR-002**: The form MUST check on submit, before calling the server: empty «البريد مطلوب»;
  malformed «البريد غير صحيح — مثل name@clinic.com». These appear under the field, with no
  banner.
- **FR-003**: Every request MUST end on one result screen: «أُرسل الطلب» and «إن كان ‹البريد›
  مسجَّلًا في العيادة فسيصله خلال دقائق رابطٌ لإعادة تعيين كلمة المرور.», with «الرجوع لتسجيل
  الدخول» — whatever the address, and whether or not anything was sent (B-R12).
- **FR-004**: A reset link MUST be emailed only when the address belongs to an active account. A
  disabled account, an invited account and an unknown address get nothing. A locked account is
  active and gets the link.
- **FR-005**: At most one reset link per account per minute MUST be sent (B-R13), and at most
  five per account in any hour; a request beyond either limit sends nothing, leaves the live
  link as it is, and is answered like any other. Both figures are platform settings.
- **FR-006**: The answers of FR-003 MUST be indistinguishable to the caller in content, form and
  response time, including when the email could not be sent.
- **FR-007**: Requests for a link MUST be limited per network address like the sign-in requests
  of spec 003, with the same warning on the form when the limit is passed.

**Links**

- **FR-008**: A link MUST carry a secret that cannot be guessed, MUST work once, and MUST be
  stored only in a form from which the secret cannot be recovered.
- **FR-009**: A reset link MUST be valid for 30 minutes from sending, and a new-account link for
  72 hours (SH-048). Both figures are platform settings.
- **FR-010**: An account MUST have at most one live link: issuing a new one replaces the earlier
  one, of either kind (B-R14).
- **FR-011**: Opening a link MUST change nothing. Only submitting a password uses the link.
- **FR-012**: The address of the page MUST stop carrying the link's secret as soon as the page
  has read it, and the secret MUST never be written to any log.
- **FR-013**: A link stops working when: it is used; its time passes; a newer link is issued for
  the account; the account's password is set by any other route; the account is disabled or
  removed.

**Setting a new password from a reset link**

- **FR-014**: A live reset link MUST open a screen titled «تعيين كلمة مرور جديدة» with «لحساب
  ‹البريد›», one field «كلمة المرور الجديدة» with the eye, the four rules, and the button «تعيين
  كلمة المرور الجديدة». The email is shown and cannot be edited.
- **FR-015**: On success the system MUST: replace the password; lift any lock and clear the
  counts that lead to one; end every session of the account; make every link of the account
  stop working; remove the "must change at first sign-in" mark; and open no session (B-R18,
  B-R19).
- **FR-016**: After success the sign-in form MUST be shown with the success banner «غُيّرت كلمة
  المرور» / «الدخول الآن بكلمة المرور الجديدة.».
- **FR-017**: After a completed reset the account's owner MUST be sent an email saying the
  password was changed, when, and to contact support if it was not them. The email contains no
  password and no link that changes anything. Nobody can switch it off.

**Activating a new account**

- **FR-018**: A live new-account link MUST open a screen titled «مرحبًا بك في ‹اسم العيادة›»
  with «اختر كلمة مرور لحسابك ‹البريد›», one field with the eye, the four rules, the note
  «الرابط صالح ٧٢ ساعة من إرساله.», and the button «تفعيل الحساب».
- **FR-019**: On success the account MUST become active with the chosen password, the link MUST
  stop working, and no session is opened. The sign-in form is shown with the success banner
  «فُعِّل الحساب» / «الدخول الآن بكلمة المرور الجديدة.».
- **FR-020**: The clinic's name MUST be shown on this screen only, in the current language. It
  MUST NOT be shown on the request form, its result, the reset screen, or the "no longer works"
  screens.

**Links that no longer work**

- **FR-021**: A reset link that no longer works MUST show «انتهت صلاحية الرابط أو استُعمل», the
  message «رابط إعادة تعيين كلمة المرور يعمل مرة واحدة ولمدة محدودة.», the button «طلب رابط
  جديد» leading to the request form, and «الرجوع لتسجيل الدخول».
- **FR-022**: A new-account link that no longer works MUST show the same title, the message
  «رابط الحساب الجديد يعمل مرة واحدة ولمدة محدودة. إرسال رابط آخر بيد من أرسل الأول.», no
  button, and «الرجوع لتسجيل الدخول» (B-R21).
- **FR-023**: The server MUST tell the two kinds of link apart so that each gets its own screen
  (SH-048). A link that was never issued, or was altered, MUST be answered exactly as a reset
  link that no longer works.
- **FR-024**: The screens of FR-021 and FR-022 MUST NOT say why the link stopped working, whose
  it was, or whether the account exists.

**The password rules**

- **FR-025**: A password MUST have at least 10 characters, an upper-case and a lower-case
  letter, a digit, and a symbol, and at most 200 characters (B-R16). The server enforces this
  wherever a password is set, whatever the screen did.
- **FR-026**: A new password MUST differ from the account's current password. This applies to a
  reset and to changing a temporary password; an account being activated has none to differ
  from.
- **FR-027**: Every screen that sets a password MUST show the four rules under the field before
  anything is typed: «١٠ أحرف على الأقل» · «حرف كبير وحرف صغير (A-z)» · «رقم واحد على الأقل
  (0-9)» · «رمز خاص واحد على الأقل (!@#$%)». A rule turns green when what is typed meets it, and
  red on submit when it does not.
- **FR-028**: A refused password MUST be shown by the red rule alone, with no second error under
  the field, and the button stays enabled (CL-SH-106). The refusal of FR-026 is the one case
  with its own message, since no rule line describes it.
- **FR-029**: No screen may have a "confirm password" field (B-R17). Every password field has
  the eye of spec 003.

**Handing over a new account**

- **FR-030**: This spec MUST provide the operations by which an account is handed over in the
  three ways of SH-044, for specs 015 and 026 to call; it delivers no screen for them:
  (a) **by link** — the account is created invited and its link is emailed;
  (b) **by generated password** — the account is created active with a password the system
  generates, returned to the caller exactly once and never retrievable again;
  (c) **by typed password** — the account is created active with a password the creator typed,
  which must meet the rules.
- **FR-031**: Accounts created by (b) or (c) MUST be marked "must change password at first
  sign-in" (CL-SH-190). A temporary password MUST stop being accepted at sign-in 72 hours after
  it was set, if it was not replaced; the refusal is the usual wrong-credentials answer and
  reveals nothing about the expiry. The figure is a platform setting.
- **FR-032**: For (a), the email MUST carry the link, the clinic's name, the sign-in address and
  the 72-hour limit. For (b) and (c), the owner MUST receive an email saying an account exists
  and giving the sign-in address, and MUST NOT contain the password (CL-SH-205).
- **FR-033**: This spec MUST provide the operation that sends a new-account link again for an
  invited account, replacing the earlier link.
- **FR-034**: When the email of FR-032, FR-033 or FR-044 cannot be sent, the operation MUST say
  so to its caller; the account remains as the operation left it.
- **FR-044**: This spec MUST provide the operation that re-issues access for an existing active
  account, for spec 026 to call, in the same three ways:
  (a) **by link** — a reset link is emailed to the owner, valid 30 minutes like any other; the
  current password keeps working until the link is used;
  (b) **by generated password** and (c) **by typed password** — the password is replaced at
  once, under the rules of FR-030 and FR-031 (returned once, marked "must change", 72 hours),
  every session of the account is ended, every link of the account stops working, any lock is
  lifted, and the owner is emailed that their access was reset by someone at the clinic and
  where to sign in — without the password.
  For an invited account, re-issuing by link is FR-033; re-issuing by password makes it active.
  A disabled account cannot be re-issued. Re-issuing by password never compares the new password
  with the current one (FR-026 does not apply): otherwise whoever re-issues could test guesses of
  a colleague's password. A link sent by re-issue is not held back by the caps of FR-005, but it
  counts toward them.

**Changing a temporary password at first sign-in**

- **FR-035**: A person whose account is marked "must change" MUST, after completing both
  sign-in steps, be shown the set-password screen before any page of the console, with a title
  saying the password is temporary, their email, one field, the four rules and one button.
- **FR-036**: While the mark stands, the server MUST refuse every request of that session except
  changing the password, reading who is signed in, renewing the session and signing out. The
  refusal is enforced on the server, not by the screen (constitution III).
- **FR-037**: On success the password is replaced, the mark is removed, the person continues in
  the same session and reaches the console. Their other sessions, if any, are ended.
- **FR-038**: There MUST be no way to skip this screen.

**Screens, records and privacy**

- **FR-039**: Every screen of this spec MUST use the frame of the sign-in screens (spec 003,
  FR-046) and exist in Arabic and English, mirrored correctly, in each of its states: request
  form, its validation, request sent; reset form, a rule refused, same-password refused; new
  account form, a rule refused; reset link no longer works; new-account link no longer works;
  temporary-password form, a rule refused, same-password refused; and, on each form, loading
  and the platform unreachable; too many requests on the forms reachable without a session. The
  two success banners are states of the sign-in form.
- **FR-040**: The action is called «إعادة تعيين كلمة المرور» wherever it is named — title,
  button and email (CL-SH-097).
- **FR-041**: These MUST be recorded for the clinic: a reset was requested (for an existing
  account); a reset was completed; an invitation was accepted (an account activated); a
  temporary password was changed; access was re-issued by someone else (with who did it and
  which way). The completed reset and the re-issue are also security events.
- **FR-042**: Passwords and link secrets MUST never be stored in a readable form, shown again,
  or written to any log; operational logs carry identifiers only, never an email address
  (SH-094).
- **FR-043**: The emails of this spec — reset link, password changed, new-account link, account
  created, access reset — cannot be switched off by anyone, and carry nothing about the
  clinic's data.

### Key Entities

- **Password link**: a one-time link emailed to an account's owner. Of one of two kinds — reset
  or new account — with the time it was sent, when it expires, and whether it was used or
  replaced. An account has at most one live link.
- **Staff account** (from spec 003): gains two facts — whether its password is temporary and must
  be changed at first sign-in, and until when a temporary password is accepted.
- **Password rule**: one of the four conditions a password must meet, each shown as its own
  line.
- **Account event**: reset requested, reset completed, invitation accepted, temporary password
  changed, access re-issued — with identifiers, time and where from. Read later by the audit trail (spec 007) and
  the clinic's activity log (spec 059).
- **Handover method**: how a new account reaches its owner — link, generated password, or typed
  password.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A person who forgot their password is signed in again in under three minutes
  without contacting anyone, of which the wait for the email is under 30 seconds in 95% of
  cases.
- **SC-002**: In an automated run over every "asking for a link" case (registered and active,
  unknown, disabled, never activated, asked again within a minute, email failing), 100% of
  answers are identical in content, and their average response times differ by less than 5%.
- **SC-003**: 0 reset emails are sent to anything but an active account, and never more than one
  per account per minute or five per account per hour.
- **SC-004**: A link is accepted at most once, and never after its time (30 minutes for a reset,
  72 hours for a new account), after a newer link, or for an account since disabled — in 100% of
  automated attempts.
- **SC-005**: After a completed reset, 100% of the account's earlier sessions are refused at
  their next request, the old password is refused, and no session was opened by the reset.
- **SC-006**: 100% of passwords that break a rule, or equal the current password, are refused by
  the server when sent directly, with nothing changed.
- **SC-007**: A person with a temporary password reaches no page and no data of the console
  before changing it — 0 successful requests other than the four allowed — and is not asked
  again at the next sign-in. A temporary password older than 72 hours opens 0 sessions.
- **SC-008**: A new team member goes from opening the invitation email to being signed in, in
  under two minutes.
- **SC-009**: 100% of the states listed in FR-039 exist in Arabic and in English, pass the
  translation check, and show a clinic's name only on the new-account screen.
- **SC-010**: 0 passwords, link secrets or email addresses appear in operational logs in the
  automated leak run extended to these screens, including the address of the page a link
  opened.
- **SC-011**: Specs 015 and 026 can hand over a new account, and re-issue access for an existing
  one, in each of the three ways without changing how links or passwords work.

## Assumptions

- Addresses follow constitution v1.1.0: the links lead to pages on the staff address,
  `app.scs-heta.com`. The BRD's sentence «إن كان ‹البريد› مسجَّلًا في العيادة» is kept as drawn,
  though one email now has one account on the whole platform.
- A new password must differ from the current one, and the owner is told by email when a reset is
  completed (product owner, 10 Oct 2026). Neither is in the BRD; both are to be added to it. No
  history of earlier passwords is kept.
- Not drawn in the design file, and built from the drawn screens' pieces: the temporary-password
  screen (its title and button wording are fixed at the page review the BRD calls for — B-O19),
  the "must be a different password" message, the too-many-requests warning on the request form,
  and the three new emails ("account created", "password changed", "access reset").
- "Too many requests" for asking for a link uses the same limit as sign-in (ten per minute per
  network address) and counts together with it.
- A reset link is live for 30 minutes and a new-account link for 72 hours, as platform
  configuration until the operators' settings screen exists (spec 024).
- A locked account may ask for and use a reset link; completing it lifts the lock (SH-049,
  B-R7).
- Completing a reset removes the "must change" mark, because the person has chosen their own
  password.
- At most five reset emails are sent to one account in an hour (product owner, 10 Oct 2026). The
  BRD has only the one-per-minute rule; this is to be added to it.
- A temporary password expires 72 hours after it was set, like the new-account link (product
  owner, 10 Oct 2026). This is not in the BRD and is to be added to it.
- The generated password is 14 characters, meets the four rules, and leaves out characters that
  look alike (such as `0` and `O`, `1` and `l`). The length is a platform setting.
- Emails are sent in Arabic and English together, as the sign-in code is, until the
  notifications engine (spec 008) owns templates and the clinic's language.
- "Whoever may send the link again" and "whoever creates an account" are decided by permissions
  in specs 005 and 026. Until then the operations are exercised by the seed and by tests.
- The events recorded here are stored in the form later specs read (007, 059); alerting anyone
  about them is spec 008.
- Changing one's own password from inside the console, when it is not temporary, is not in the
  BRD's sign-in chapter and belongs with the security settings (spec 085).
- How link secrets are generated and kept, and how the "must change" restriction is carried by
  the session, are fixed at planning under the constitution; they are deliberately not named
  here.
